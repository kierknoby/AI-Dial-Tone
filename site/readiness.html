PRE-CALL READINESS / THE CENTRAL PRINCIPLE

Can it actually
make the call?

The question to answer before wasting a human's time or consuming a paid attempt. Check the route first, report what was observed, and never confuse a reachable PBX with a guaranteed completed call.

FROM NATIVE DIAL-TONE

Qualify first.
Then commit.

The Windows Dial-Tone project improved its MicroSIP roots by giving operators a pre-call qualification check, an audible local dial tone when checks succeed, and detailed Call Trace reporting. That experience shapes the AI version: a bot should receive structured readiness evidence rather than an unexplained green light.

For a bot, audible feedback becomes machine-readable evidence. An isolated echo or tone test can test the local audio path without calling a person. The production design must link each observation to its authenticated PBX source and permitted route.

WHAT A PREFLIGHT CAN SUPPORT
  • Account registration and configured PJSIP endpoint state
  • Recent authenticated SIP OPTIONS response and latency
  • TLS certificate and mandatory SRTP/DTLS-SRTP policy
  • Approved route class, codec and audio-path prerequisites
  • Internal tone/echo media on an isolated, non-human test destination
WHAT IT CANNOT PROMISE
  • That the selected carrier route will accept the INVITE
  • That the destination will ring, answer or be a real person
  • That remote audio or dialogue will be intelligible
  • That a booking or task will actually be completed
  • That a previously valid route cannot change before origination

A STRUCTURED RESPONSE, NOT A GUESS

Readiness has
levels of evidence.

Local endpoint and network checks happen before a live call. Real carrier delivery, two-way media and the recipient's response become known only later. These statuses are a design proposal, not a deployed hosted API.

01 / LOCAL

Endpoint observed

PBX registration, OPTIONS, transport and codec prerequisites, tied to fresh observations.

02 / POLICY

Permitted path candidate

Destination class and route permissions checked by an operator-controlled policy.

03 / MEDIA

Non-human media proof

An isolated tone/echo test may provide measured audio feedback without calling a person.

04 / CALL

Observed call outcome

Actual answer, RTP evidence and hangup are recorded only once a real connection is attempted.

Important: 0.0.1 does not implement all these gates. The BYOT connector currently has a read-only ARI endpoint-state check; the Windows-native readiness implementation has not been ported or independently validated on Asterisk.

THE DESIGN QUESTION

No unnecessary
human interruption.

Test the internal path using non-human destinations first. If registration, qualification or routing policy is uncertain, block the attempt and give the bot a specific, actionable reason rather than a fictional success.
