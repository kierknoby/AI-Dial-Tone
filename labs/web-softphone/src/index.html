AI Dial-Tone Browser SIP Lab

A browser telephone and opt-in bot-media playground. Not for production or PSTN calling.

DISCONNECTED

01 · Dedicated SIP test account

Use only an isolated Asterisk lab extension. No external or chargeable dialplan routes.

02 · Test call

Call requests are restricted to locally approved extensions; a 60-second browser timer requests hangup. The PBX must enforce its own independent cutoff.

03 · Operator authority

Active grants: Not applied

These are client-side safety checks, not a PBX authorisation boundary.

04 · Authenticated bot controller

Mutual HMAC authentication over WSS. Never send your pairing secret inside the hello message. Pairing does not give a bot your SIP password.

Bot connection: OFFLINE

05 · Bot voice transport

Telephone audio → bot → telephone audio, PCM16 mono, 16 kHz, 20-ms frames.

Audio pipeline: OFF

Requires operator approval, a paired bot, and an answered call. Bot must also send audio.enable. This replaces the outgoing microphone track until stopped. Bluetooth, Safari, and SIP.js peer-connection access require real-device testing.

06 · Readiness

Not checked

Registration and local microphone readiness do not prove SIP OPTIONS response, ICE connectivity, PSTN reachability, or speech quality.

07 · Event and call trace



This browser SIP experiment is separate from any future private calling backend; no public calling service is operating. No x402, identity assurance, billing, production bot service, or PSTN entitlement here. Browser testing only.
